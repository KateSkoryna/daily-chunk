# TabBar

Main navigation: WORDS (home, add words) · LEARN (today's lesson) · REVIEW (due cards, badge = count) · ME (word bank, stats, settings).

- Mobile: bottom, 4 equal columns, `size-tabbar` 56px + safe-area inset, top border 2px `line`, labels `pixel` 22px letter-spacing 2px; active `yellow`, others `ink-muted`; badge `pink` fill.
- Desktop ≥1024px: becomes a left rail, `size-rail` 240px wide, `bg-100`, right border 2px `line`; items 56px high, padding 0 24px, active item gets a `yellow` label and a `bg-200` row; app title in `display-l` at the top of the rail.
