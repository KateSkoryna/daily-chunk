# GapFill

A German sentence from the story with one blank; the learner picks or types the missing form.

- Sentence `body` (Plex 17/26); blank = min 110px, 3px `cyan` underline; RU hint below in `body-s` `ink-muted`.
- Options: 2×2 grid, 8px gap, 56px high, `bg-100`, 2px `line-strong`, Plex 600 18px. Correct = cyan border + ✓; wrong = pink border + ✗ and the correct one turns cyan.
- Typing mode (Boss round, harder lessons): the blank becomes an input with a 2px cyan border, 48px high; accept umlaut alternatives (ae/oe/ue/ss) and show the correct spelling.
- Sentence-build variant: word tiles 48px high, `bg-100`, 2px `line-strong`, tapped tiles move into the answer line.
